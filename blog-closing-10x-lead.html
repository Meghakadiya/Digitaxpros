Closing the 10x Lead: Scripts for Agency Owners

MR
Marcus Reynolds
Head of Growth

There's a critical difference between closing a $500/mo client and a $5,000/mo client. Most agencies fail to scale because they use the exact same script for both tiers, treating high-end accounts like commodity purchases.

Stop Pitching Deliverables

A $500/mo client asks: "How many blog posts will I get?" A $5,000/mo client asks: "What will my ROI be in 6 months?" If you are discussing line items like backlinks, SEO audits, or ad creatives with a 10x client, you have already lost. You need to pitch the destination, not the vehicle.

The 10x Question

"Mr. Prospect, assuming we hit our growth targets and double your qualified inbound calls within 90 days, do you actually have the operational capacity to fulfill that volume?"

The Diagnostic Call Script

Before proposing any price, you must establish the financial gap. Use this exact flow:

  1. Where are they now? "What is your current monthly revenue strictly from new leads?"
  2. Where do they want to be? "Where do you need that number to be in 12 months for this year to be a success?"
  3. The Gap: "So there is a $40k/mo gap between where you are and where you need to be. What is stopping you from reaching that on your own?"

Conclusion

When you position yourself as a strategic partner who solves a specific $40k/mo revenue gap, presenting a $5k/mo retainer becomes a mathematical bargain, rather than an expensive expense.
