The Ultimate Checklist for Google Maps Domination

DJ
David Johnson
Lead SEO Strategist

Ranking in the top 3 spots in Google Maps (the "Local Pack") isn't a vanity metric—it's the driving force behind foot traffic and phone calls for any local service business. So how do you beat the established giants in your town?

The Core Algorithm

Google Maps ranks businesses based on three primary factors: Relevance, Distance, and Prominence. While you can't control distance, you have absolute control over relevance and prominence. The goal is to build an unshakeable profile of trust that Google cannot ignore.

Priority Focus

Reviews and exact Name-Address-Phone (NAP) consistency represent the highest weighted factors for outranking competitors in dense metropolitan areas.

1. Complete Every Blank Field

A staggering number of business owners fail to complete their Google Business Profile fully. Add all relevant categories, describe your business in a few keyword-rich paragraphs, add open hours (and holiday hours!), and specifically list out all services you offer.

2. The 100 Photo Strategy

Google's machine learning vision APIs love localized user-generated content. You should aim to upload new photos consistently. Exterior shots, interior shots, team members working, and especially photos from job sites (with GPS EXIF data intact) are powerful signals.

Conclusion

Google Maps domination requires weekly maintenance. By consistently managing reviews, feeding the profile new photos, and aggressively hunting down localized citations, you will eventually capture the top 3 placement in your service area.
